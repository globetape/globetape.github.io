Iowa Lead Azide Detonation: 4,500 kg Destroyed | World News, Nov 25

Middletown · 26 November 2026 · 1:54

The US Army destroyed about 4,500 kilograms of degraded lead azide at an Iowa ammunition plant, and early EPA readings showed no airborne lead.

◀◀ DEEP DIVE · 2 MIN READ

World news on the map: 25 November 2026

The international headlines of 25 November 2026, mapped from west to east.

United States · Iowa: Army detonates unstable explosive

We start in Iowa, where the US Army blew up its stockpile of lead azide. The degraded explosive risked going off on its own as temperatures dropped, so about 4,500 kilograms were destroyed. Early EPA readings showed no airborne lead, but some residents fear lasting contamination.

Brazil · Trade: Brazil rejects new us tariff plan

Now to Brazil, where Washington proposed new tariffs on aircraft and sugar. That includes a 10 percent duty on finished planes made by Embraer. President Lula's office called the move unacceptable. It wants a negotiated deal, but does not rule out reciprocal measures.

Poland · Defence: Poland plans border no-fly zone

Now Poland, which plans a no-fly zone along its eastern border with Ukraine. Deputy minister Cezary Tomczyk said it would help pilots down Russian missiles and drones. He hopes it will be in place by early January. Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski calls downing missiles over Ukraine self-defence; NATO is not in favour.

South Africa · Courts: Court voids phala phala report

In South Africa, President Cyril Ramaphosa won a court ruling on Wednesday. Judges in Cape Town set aside a 2022 panel report that paved the way to impeachment. It concerned cash stolen from his farm; the court said the panel overstepped. That halts the impeachment inquiry based on it.

Egypt · Cairo: Fuel truck fire kills 31 in cairo

Then Cairo, where a fuel truck overturned and burst into flames on the Ring Road. Egypt's health ministry says at least 31 people were killed. Prosecutors say 27 vehicles burned and have opened an investigation. Footage showed a huge fireball over the Al-Warraq neighbourhood.

North Korea · Switzerland: North korea shuts swiss office

Finally, Pyongyang, where North Korea has shut Switzerland's new diplomatic office. State media say the Swiss envoy had 48 hours to leave. The row is over Swiss sanctions on a children's camp; Bern regrets the move and is sending its envoy to Beijing.
