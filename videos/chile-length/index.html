Laying Chile Over Europe: A Country Longer Than Lisbon to Moscow

Chile is 4,270 km long from north to south, yet on average only 177 km wide. Lay it over Europe with its southern tip on Lisbon and it does not stop at Moscow, 3,907 km away: it keeps going for about another 360 km, passing over ten countries on the way.

How long is Chile?

Chile measures 4,270 km from its northern border to its southern tip, according to the Library of Congress country study. Its average width is just 177 km, and even at its widest it reaches only about 350 km.

That shape is hard to grasp on a normal map, where Chile looks like a thin ribbon and is easy to dismiss as small. Its length tells a different story.

What happens if you put Chile over Europe?

To make that length easier to picture, we took Chile's real outline and moved it across the Atlantic without stretching it, then laid it over Europe.

  • Its southern tip lands on Lisbon.
  • From there it climbs through Galicia in Spain, crosses Brittany in France, then Belgium and northern Germany.
  • It continues over Poland and Belarus.
  • It passes right over Moscow.
  • Then it keeps going for about 360 km beyond the Russian capital.

The outline is not a straight line. Chile is slightly arched, so when it lies across Europe it bends north through Galicia, Brittany and Belgium instead of following the straight route from Lisbon to Moscow.

Along the way, the cities that fall inside the outline include Lisbon, Cologne, Berlin, Vilnius, Minsk, Moscow and Vladimir.

How many countries does Chile cover in Europe?

Ten. Measured on Natural Earth outlines, Chile's shape covers part of Russia (including Kaliningrad), Germany, France, Belarus, Poland, Lithuania, Portugal, Spain, Belgium and the Netherlands.

That is ten European countries sitting under one long, thin South American country.

Is Chile longer than Lisbon to Moscow?

Yes. The straight-line distance from Lisbon to Moscow is 3,907 km. Chile is 4,270 km long, so the difference is about 360 km. When we placed Chile's outline on the map, its northern tip ended up just over 360 km past Moscow, which matches the official figures.

Is Chile longer than Europe is wide?

No, and this is a claim worth correcting. Measured from Cabo da Roca in Portugal to the Ural Mountains, Europe spans roughly 5,200 to 5,460 km, depending on where along the Urals you stop. Chile, at 4,270 km, is shorter than that.

So Chile does not stretch across all of Europe. But it is easily longer than the trip from Lisbon to Moscow, which is already a very long way.

Key takeaways

  • Chile is 4,270 km long and only 177 km wide on average.
  • Laid over Europe from Lisbon, it passes over Moscow and continues about 360 km further.
  • The straight-line distance from Lisbon to Moscow is 3,907 km.
  • Chile's outline covers ten European countries, from Portugal to Russia.
  • Europe, at about 5,200 to 5,460 km from west to east, is still wider than Chile is long.
