Quebec Religious Symbols Ban Upheld | World News, Nov 27

Quebec City · 28 November 2026 · 1:58

THE SHORT ANSWER

Canada's Supreme Court largely upheld Quebec's Bill 21, the 2019 law that bars teachers, police and other public workers from wearing religious symbols such as hijabs, turbans, kippahs and crosses on the job.

The November 27 world news briefing maps the Costa Rica airport standoff over UN rapporteur Francesca Albanese, Ukraine's Black Sea ceasefire offer, the latest Shakahola cult verdict in Kenya, Russia's Zircon-armed submarine Perm and the US strike that disabled a ship leaving Iran.

◀◀ DEEP DIVE · 2 MIN READ

World news on the map: 27 November 2026

The international headlines of 27 November 2026, mapped from west to east.

Costa Rica · Un: Un expert stuck at airport

We start in Costa Rica, where a UN rapporteur spent a second night in an airport. Officials refused Francesca Albanese entry, citing an unexplained international alert. She denies any link to terrorism and says she will leave. A court gave immigration 24 hours to justify the decision.

Canada · Supreme Court: Top court upholds quebec ban

Now to Canada, where the Supreme Court largely upheld Quebec's religious symbols ban. It bars teachers, police and other public workers from wearing hijabs, turbans, kippahs or crosses. One part, on face coverings for elected members, was struck down. Prime Minister Carney said Ottawa won't use the same override.

Ukraine · Black Sea: Kyiv offers black sea ceasefire

Then the Black Sea, where Ukraine offered a ceasefire to protect grain exports. Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said Kyiv is now waiting for Russia's answer. He said Moscow wants oil included; Kyiv says that belongs in a wider energy truce. Attacks on ships have disrupted Odesa's exports.

Kenya · Shakahola Case: Cult leader guilty over 123 deaths

Now to Mombasa, on Kenya's coast. A court found cult leader Paul Mackenzie guilty of manslaughter over 123 more deaths. His followers starved in the Shakahola forest, where more than 400 bodies were found. He is already serving 200 years for murder. His lawyer plans an appeal.

Russia · Navy: Russia commissions submarine perm

North to Severodvinsk, where Vladimir Putin watched Russia's navy commission the nuclear submarine Perm. The Kremlin says it is the first with Zircon hypersonic missiles as standard. Putin said the Perm will join the Pacific Fleet. He said five more missile submarines are due by 2030.

Iran · Gulf Of Oman: Us disables ship in iran blockade

Finally, the Gulf of Oman, where the US military says it disabled a merchant ship. It says the Pacific Archer left Bandar Abbas and tried to run the US blockade. A strike hit its stern; the crew was unharmed. Iran's navy chief vowed to break the blockade.
