◀◀ DEEP DIVE · 2 MIN READ

World news on the map: 23 November 2026

The international headlines of 23 November 2026, mapped from west to east.

United States · Tech: Nvidia reinstates jensen huang

We start in Santa Clara, California, where Nvidia brought back its chief executive. The board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang, who resigned on November 12. Nvidia shares closed more than 31 percent higher on Monday. The board says an independent investigation will continue.

Colombia · Security: Drones hit police base in cúcuta

Now to Cúcuta, in northeastern Colombia, near the border with Venezuela. Explosive drones targeted the regional police command before dawn on Monday. Officials say no officers were hurt, but three civilians, including a baby, were injured. More than 60 homes were damaged.

Poland · Nato: Nato sends awacs to poland

Then Poland, where NATO is answering missiles that crashed inside the country. Polish prosecutors say three Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles came down on Friday. After a NATO meeting on Monday, Poland said allies will provide an AWACS early-warning plane. It helps detect low-flying missiles and drones.

Libya · Ukraine War: Ukraine claims warship sunk

South to Benghazi, in eastern Libya. Ukraine's president says seven Ukrainian-made sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury there on Sunday. He says a helicopter on board was destroyed and a Russian cargo ship was hit. The UN mission in Libya called for an independent investigation.

India · Nepal: India pledges $1.6bn to nepal

In New Delhi, India pledged 1.6 billion dollars to Nepal. Narendra Modi announced it after talks with Nepal's new prime minister, Balendra Shah. Nepal had asked for two billion, after floods that killed more than 1,400 people. India also agreed to give Nepal access to two more ports.

Indonesia · Factory Fire: 24 dead in candy factory fire

Now to Indonesia, west of Jakarta, in Banten province. Officials confirmed on Monday that 24 people died in a fire at a candy factory in Cikande. About 30 workers are still missing. Early reports point to a short circuit in an air conditioner.

South Korea · Air Defence: Russian jets trigger island alert

Last stop: South Korea, where an island got an air-raid alert. Seoul says Russian warplanes entered its air defence zone early Tuesday, local time, and neared Ulleung Island. Fighter jets scrambled; the alert lasted minutes. Seoul says its airspace was not violated, and will protest to Russia.
