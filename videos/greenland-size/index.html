◀◀ DEEP DIVE · 3 MIN READ

Greenland's Real Size: Smaller Than Algeria

Greenland covers 2,166,086 km². That makes it the world's largest island, but it is still smaller than Algeria, which covers 2,381,740 km². On the world map most of us grew up with, though, Greenland looks nearly 12 times bigger than Algeria. The reason is the map, not the island.

How big is Greenland really?

According to the CIA World Factbook, Greenland has a total area of 2,166,086 km², and about 1.71 million km² of it lies under an ice sheet. It is the largest island on Earth: Australia is bigger, but it counts as a continent.

So Greenland is huge for an island. It is just not the giant it appears to be on a classic world map.

Why does Greenland look so big on maps?

Most world maps online, including Google Maps, use a version of the Mercator projection, drawn by Gerardus Mercator in 1569 for sailors. To flatten a round planet into a rectangle while keeping shapes and angles correct, Mercator stretches everything more and more the further it is from the equator.

The stretch is precise: on a Mercator map, area grows with the square of the secant of the latitude. At 60° north an area is shown about 4 times bigger than it really is; past 72° north, more than 10 times.

Greenland sits between roughly 60° and 84° north, so almost all of it is drawn in the most stretched part of the map. Measured on the same map, Greenland's outline covers on average about 16 times its real area. Algeria, much closer to the equator, is inflated only about 1.3 times.

That is why, on the map, Greenland looks nearly 12 times bigger than Algeria.

What happens if you move Greenland to Algeria's latitude?

To compare them fairly, we took Greenland's real outline, moved it across the globe without stretching it, and set it down in the Atlantic, right next to Algeria and at the same latitude.

  • At that latitude both shapes are stretched by the same amount.
  • On the map, Greenland's outline now covers about 0.92 of Algeria's area, close to the real ratio.
  • In other words, once the distortion is the same for both, Algeria is clearly the bigger one.

We also checked whether Greenland could fit inside Algeria. It cannot: Greenland is long and narrow, and even in its best position only about 81% of it fits within Algeria's borders. That is why the video puts it beside Algeria rather than on top of it.

Is Greenland bigger than Mexico?

Yes. Mexico covers 1,964,375 km², so Greenland is about 10% bigger. Greenland may be smaller than Algeria, but it is still bigger than many large countries.

Why it matters

Mercator maps are excellent for navigation, because a straight line on them keeps a constant compass bearing. They are much worse at comparing sizes. Anything far from the equator, like Greenland, Canada or northern Russia, looks bigger than it really is, while countries near the equator look smaller.

The simple rule: when two places sit at very different latitudes, do not trust a Mercator map to tell you which one is bigger. Move them to the same latitude, or check the numbers.

So next time Greenland looks huge on your map, remember: it is the world's largest island, and still smaller than Algeria.
