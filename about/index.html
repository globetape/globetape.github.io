■ ABOUT

We rewind the map.

Every place on Earth has a story on tape. We hit rewind. Globe Tape tells map stories: wars and moving borders, countries inside other countries, the true size of things, and the history behind every line on the map.

How we make them

Every video starts with one question and a script written for it. We start at the end, show where the map is today, then hit rewind and watch it change, date by date.

Real map data

The maps are real data, animated with our own map engine: NASA Blue Marble satellite imagery (public domain), real terrain elevation from AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen) and Natural Earth borders (public domain). Areas and distances are measured on the globe, not on a flat map. Front lines and historical borders come from dated sources credited on each video page. Flags are drawn from flag-icons (MIT). Narration is a synthetic voice.

Sources

Every date, figure and border is checked against sources before it goes on screen. The sources are named on each video page and in each YouTube description.

Neutrality

On wars and disputes we show what happened and when. No sides taken.

Corrections policy

Spotted a mistake? Tell us in the comments on YouTube. We check it, and if you're right we pin a correction under the video.
