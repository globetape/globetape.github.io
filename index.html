? FAQ

Questions

What is Globe Tape?

Globe Tape is a YouTube channel (@globetape) of animated map stories: wars and moving borders, countries inside other countries, the true size of places and the history behind lines on the map. Each video starts at the end and rewinds to explain how the map got that way.

How accurate are the maps?

The maps are built from real data: NASA Blue Marble satellite imagery, real terrain elevation (AWS Terrain Tiles) and Natural Earth borders. Areas and distances are measured on the globe, not on a flat projection, and every date and figure is checked against named sources listed with each video.

Does Globe Tape take sides on wars and border disputes?

No. On wars and disputes we show what happened and when, with dates and sources, and leave opinions out.

What if a video has a mistake?

Tell us in the YouTube comments. We check it, and if you're right we pin a correction under the video.

How often are new videos released?

New Shorts arrive several times a week, and longer deep-dive features with chapters come regularly. Subscribe on YouTube or follow the feed on this site to get every new tape.
