Kaliningrad: Why Does Russia Own It?

Former name
Königsberg
Until 1945 the city was German, part of East Prussia.
Transferred to the USSR
1945
The Potsdam Agreement of August 1, 1945 placed Königsberg and the northern part of East Prussia under the Soviet Union.
SOURCE: Potsdam Agreement (1945)
Renamed Kaliningrad
July 4, 1946
The city was renamed after Mikhail Kalinin.
Cut off from mainland Russia
1991
The USSR recognised Lithuania's independence on September 6, 1991, and the Soviet Union itself dissolved in December 1991.
Neighbours in NATO and the EU
By 2004
Poland joined NATO in 1999 and Lithuania on March 29, 2004; both joined the EU on May 1, 2004.
SOURCE: NATO and EU accession records
Ice-free port
Baltiysk
Home of Russia's Baltic Fleet and Russia's only Baltic port that stays free of ice all year.
SOURCE: US Naval Institute
Area and population
About 15,100 km² · about 1 million people
A small exclave compared with the rest of Russia.

Russia Sold Alaska for 2 Cents an Acre

Purchase price
$7,200,000
The sum the United States agreed to pay Russia for Alaska in 1867.
SOURCE: US Office of the Historian
Treaty signed
March 30, 1867
Signed in Washington by US Secretary of State William Seward and Russian minister Eduard de Stoeckl, at 4 a.m. after negotiating through the night.
SOURCE: US Office of the Historian
Senate vote
37 to 2
The US Senate ratified the treaty on April 9, 1867.
SOURCE: US Office of the Historian
Formal handover
October 18, 1867
In Sitka, the Russian flag was lowered and the US flag was raised.
SOURCE: US Office of the Historian
Price per acre
About 2 cents
Roughly 1,518,800 km² (375 million acres) for $7.2 million works out to about 1.9 cents an acre.
Size compared with France
About 2.7 times
Alaska has about 1,481,346 km² of land, against about 543,940 km² for metropolitan France.
Prudhoe Bay oil discovery
Announced March 12, 1968
Prudhoe Bay is the largest oil field in the United States.
SOURCE: National Park Service
Alaska oil output in 2026
About 413,000–428,000 barrels a day
At around $100 a barrel, that is roughly $7 million worth of oil every four hours.
SOURCE: US Energy Information Administration

How Much of Ukraine Does Russia Control?

Share of Ukraine controlled by Russia (17 Nov 2026)
About 19%
19.2% of Ukraine including Crimea, based on the DeepStateMap snapshot of 17 November 2026.
SOURCE: DeepStateMap (deepstatemap.live), monthly snapshots to 17 Nov 2026
Area controlled by Russia (17 Nov 2026)
About 114,000 km²
114,449 km² in the 17 November 2026 snapshot, more than the whole of Portugal (92,225 km²).
SOURCE: DeepStateMap (deepstatemap.live), monthly snapshots to 17 Nov 2026
Annexation of Crimea
18 March 2014
The date the treaty annexing Crimea to Russia was signed.
SOURCE: Britannica
Fighting in the Donbas
April 2014
Russian troops and Russian-backed local forces took part of the Donbas; the line there was stable from February 2015.
SOURCE: Britannica
Full-scale invasion
24 February 2022
Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine.
SOURCE: Britannica
Peak of Russian control (March 2022)
Over a fifth
Estimates range from about 21% (around 133,000 km²) to 27% depending on the source.
SOURCE: ISW
Share controlled by Russia (December 2022)
17.7%
After Ukraine retook ground around Kyiv, Kharkiv and Kherson in 2022, the figure rose slowly to 19.2% by November 2026.
SOURCE: DeepStateMap (deepstatemap.live), monthly snapshots to 17 Nov 2026

The True Size of Africa: US, India and EU Fit

Area of Africa
About 30.4 million km²
Estimates range from 30,365,000 to 30,370,000 km² depending on the source.
SOURCE: Britannica
Area of Greenland
2,166,086 km²
On a Mercator map Greenland looks about as big as Africa.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
Africa compared with Greenland
14 times bigger
Africa's area divided by Greenland's comes out at about 14.
SOURCE: Britannica
Mercator projection
1569
Drawn by Gerardus Mercator for navigators; it enlarges areas about 4 times at 60° latitude and about 10.5 times at 72°.
Mainland United States
8,080,464 km²
The 48 contiguous states plus DC, without Alaska or Hawaii.
SOURCE: US Census Bureau
India
3,287,263 km²
The second country placed inside Africa in the comparison.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
European Union (27 countries)
4,236,351 km²
All 27 member states fit inside Africa together with the US and India.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
Share of Africa still uncovered
Almost half
The three together cover 15,604,078 km², about 51% of Africa, leaving about 49% free.
SOURCE: fit measured on Natural Earth outlines

Lesotho: The Country Inside South Africa

Area of Lesotho
30,355 km²
A fully independent country, completely surrounded by South Africa.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
Area of Belgium, for comparison
30,689 km²
Lesotho and Belgium are almost exactly the same size.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
Lowest point in Lesotho
About 1,400 m
The highest lowest point of any country in the world.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
British protection
1868
King Moshoeshoe I asked Britain for protection, and the territory became the protectorate of Basutoland.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
Union of South Africa
1910
Basutoland stayed outside the Union of South Africa and never joined it.
Independence
4 October 1966
Basutoland became the independent country of Lesotho.
SOURCE: CIA World Factbook
Countries entirely surrounded by one other country
3
Lesotho, San Marino and Vatican City.
